Privacy Policy

How Tivoro handles enquiry and tool data.

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Information We Collect

Tivoro may collect details you share through forms, WhatsApp links and AI tools, including name, mobile number, email, student stage, parent requirement, business information, website URL, city, state and your selected service interest.

How We Use It

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, suggest a suitable course or business service, prepare a counselling or service discussion, improve our tools and understand what visitors need from Tivoro.

Website Tools

When you use tools such as Website Readiness Checker or Digital Trust Score, Tivoro checks only public website information needed to create a basic readiness report. These tools are guidance tools and are not a security audit.

Third-Party Services

Some enquiries may be sent through Google Forms or WhatsApp. Tivoro may also use basic analytics to understand page performance. These services process data under their own policies.

Children and Students

For child learning guidance, parents or guardians should share information on behalf of the child. We ask only for details needed to understand the learning requirement and suggest a practical next step.

Data Requests

You can request correction or deletion of your enquiry details by emailing tivorosupport@gmail.com.
